# Creative Mechanisms — Analyst Reference V0.1

## CM-001 Hook
Record attention_trigger(s) and expression_format(s) separately, both multi-label. Examples: curiosity gap, emotional longing, relatable need, identity recognition, belief challenge, surprise, local-language joke; expressed through photo carousel, POV, screenshot, dialogue, meme, voiced skit, trend. The first 3 seconds / first image should create a legible reason to continue, not necessarily explain the full story. Curiosity is useful when the viewer knows what answer they want; confusion is not curiosity. Do not conflate hook watch metrics with registration potential.

## CM-002 Reverse & Payoff
Record setup_expectation, reveal, reveal_position, emotional_payoff and links_to_motivation. Reverse types can include narrative, emotional, cognitive, product, micro-reversals and others. May be none. Quality depends on setup, surprise, payoff, timing and how much viewing incentive exists before reveal. Reverse can be crucial to both engagement and download motivation when aligned. No automatic 70% rubric coefficient.

## CM-003 Motivation
Analyze user_desire -> popup_association -> personal_possibility. Identify the user's plausible first-person thought, not 'resonates with audience'. Supported forms: romantic aspiration; emotional connection; humorous social interaction; direct social need; belief transformation; friendship/belonging; other. Allow combined types. Distinguish factual authenticity from emotional authenticity, local credibility, and experience plausibility. A video can be staged yet its potential product experience feel achievable. Never mislabel staged content as a genuine user story.

Motivation alignment: whether the reason to watch overlaps the reason to use Popup. Timing: first point motivation is established; reinforcement: how later content strengthens/weakens it. For long challenge content, people may enjoy the performer rather than desire the app. For short POVs, payoff may be appropriate closer to the ending when still reached.

## CM-004 Trends and localization
First assess content expression. Trend status, language nuances, sound and localized meme literacy are optional contextual enablers. Trend adoption may be direct translation, adaptation, or reinvention; none intrinsically earns higher marks. When unable to understand local homophones/idioms, disclose uncertainty rather than inventing a joke.

## CM-005 Product integration
Record visibility/placement, narrative function, and motivation connection separately. Screenshots as story venue and casual app references as need reminders can both convert. Deep placement, early logo or hard CTA are NOT mandatory.

## CM-006 Replication diagnosis
Compare format replication, mechanism replication and motivation replication separately. At low spend (< plausible learning volume) mark outcomes 'no scale / inconclusive', not 'proven creative failure'. Compare against original in similar test conditions where possible.

## CM-007 Watch vs registration
Use the funnel: impression -> 2s/6s watch -> deeper watch/CTR -> registration -> CPA. Video and photo ads can have different metric availability and platform semantics. Aggregate audience measures cannot locate a particular second-by-second drop-off without retention curves. Avoid causal language based on correlations alone.
