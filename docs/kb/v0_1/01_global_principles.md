# Content Lab — Global Principles V0.1
Status: working draft, based on human expert feedback; not a trained predictive model.
Primary outcome: Unique Registrations for Popup. Secondary outcomes: CPA, weekly registrations, effective paid-ad lifespan, target-audience quality. CTR, views, watch metrics are diagnostic rather than targets.

GP-001 Outcome over format. No mandatory winning template: narrative romance, memes, humor, POVs, and direct-need videos may work. Do not punish a missing reverse in a non-reverse creative.
GP-002 Separate independent estimates: Creative Score (quality of content expression) and Predicted Registration Potential (motivation to use Popup). Real ad outcomes belong in a separate evidence field. Neither estimate may be inferred from known results.
GP-003 Hook, Reverse/Payoff and Motivation must be analyzed in relation to one another. Reverse is optional, but engaging entertainment is not by itself evidence of app-download intent.
GP-004 Ask separately: why keep watching, what was rewarding, why try Popup? People may admire a performer without wanting the product.
GP-005 Judge market-relative: same language, target audience, format, channel, and comparable spend/time cohorts whenever possible. Transferable human needs can work across regions; localization and trends are aids, not obligatory quality criteria.
GP-006 Timing matters: motivation must arrive while a plausible share of viewers remain; for longer content evaluate periodic rewards and sustained product relevance. Do not infer individual retention from aggregate rates.
GP-007 Replication is separate from performance: low-cost screenshot formats scale production, not necessarily success rates. Preserve the full Hook+Reverse+Motivation narrative for tested template replications when the team chooses; also explore novel mechanisms.
GP-008 Label evidence: OBSERVED_MEDIA, ADS_DATA, HUMAN_INSIGHT, AGENT_HYPOTHESIS, MISSING. Never turn a proposed explanation into a causal finding.
GP-009 Registration peak, sustained volume and CPA trajectory are separate. Organic lifetime views do not establish ad lifetime.
GP-010 Learn from exceptions, including direct-need memes without reverse, long-lived local-language humor, and entertainment-first failures.
GP-011 Perceived experience plausibility does not require documentary authenticity. Fabricated/AI/meme material may still evoke credible product possibility. Never claim invented scenarios are verified testimonials.
GP-012 No universally fixed weights. Expert considers reverse extremely important (roughly '70%' as subjective emphasis when central), and perceived realism similarly important to motivation; these are NOT statistical effect estimates or fixed score coefficients.
